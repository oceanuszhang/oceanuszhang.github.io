Data Science Intern at **[Zymo Research Corp](https://www.zymoresearch.com/)**, Department of Integrated Omics, under Kyle McClary, PhD. (May 2024 – May 2025)

### AWS Neptune Knowledge Graph

Assisted in building an **AWS Neptune graph database** linking researchers, diseases, microbiomes, and publications. The graph enables complex relational queries across the microbiome research landscape to support literature discovery and product positioning.

### Personalized Diet Profiling Pipeline

Created a **Python pipeline** for personalized diet profiling using Healthy Eating Index (HEI) food groups and scaled energy intake. The pipeline ingests dietary recall data and outputs individualized nutrition profiles aligned with microbiome study design requirements.

### Machine Learning for Customer Insights

Developed **machine learning models** predicting customer product purchases with **75% accuracy**.

- Feature engineering from purchase history, search behavior, and product metadata
- Model evaluation with ROC/AUC, precision-recall analysis
- Integrated external APIs to generate targeted mailing insights
- Results secured **$32,000 in revenue within one week** of deployment

**Tools:** Python, AWS Neptune (graph DB), scikit-learn, pandas, Gremlin query language, REST APIs
