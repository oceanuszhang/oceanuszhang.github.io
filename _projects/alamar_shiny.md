Bioinformatics Intern II at **[Alamar Biosciences](https://alamarbio.com/)**, Department of Bioinformatics, under Joanne Beer, PhD. (May 2025 – Dec 2025)

### Plate Randomization Dashboard

Designed and built an **R Shiny dashboard** with Plotly visualizations to assist scientists in randomizing sample placement across plates for large-scale proteomics experiments. Randomized plate design reduces batch effects that can confound downstream analysis.

- Interactive visualization of plate layouts with color-coded sample groupings
- Automated randomization algorithms accounting for experimental covariates
- Deployed through **AWS** for internal use

### Statistical Power & Sample Size Calculator

Developed a customer-facing **interactive R Shiny Web App** for statistical power and sample size calculations under multiple-testing correction frameworks. The tool supports experimental design planning for large-scale omics studies.

- Supports multiple hypothesis testing corrections (Bonferroni, BH/FDR)
- Inputs: effect size, alpha, power, number of tests
- Generates downloadable reports for study planning
- Deployed for external customer access

### Assay Reproducibility Analysis

Conducted comprehensive **correlation analysis** of signal quality metrics, environmental factors, and sample stability data to identify root causes of plate effects and improve assay reproducibility.

**Tools:** R, R Shiny, Plotly, AWS, ggplot2
