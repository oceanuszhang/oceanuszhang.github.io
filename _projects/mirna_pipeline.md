Graduate research at **[Emory University School of Medicine](https://med.emory.edu/)** under Aliza Wingo & Thomas Wingo, MD, PhD.

### Overview

Sex differences in miRNA expression may underlie differential vulnerability to neurodegenerative diseases including Alzheimer's disease (AD), Depression, and Mild Cognitive Impairment (MCI). This project developed a reproducible computational pipeline to characterize these differences.

### Methods

- Developed a reproducible **voom-limma pipeline** in R to analyze miRNA expression data from brain tissue
- Modeled **sex-specific effects** in patients across neurodegenerative disease subgroups (AD, Depression, MCI)
- Performed **outlier detection** using PCA and hierarchical clustering
- Conducted **GSEA** and produced publication-quality visualizations for AD and MCI subgroups
- Drafted manuscript for the Emory MPH Applied Practical Experience (APE) committee review

### Tools

R, limma, voom, edgeR, ggplot2, clusterProfiler
