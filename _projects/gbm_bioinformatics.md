Capstone project at **[Emory University School of Public Health](https://sph.emory.edu/)** under the supervision of Xiangqin Cui, PhD and Christopher Willey, MD, PhD.

### Overview

Glioblastoma multiforme (GBM) is one of the most aggressive brain tumors. This project analyzed gene expression changes in GBM patient-derived xenolines under hypoxia and normoxia conditions to identify pathway-level mechanisms of adaptation.

### Methods

- Processed bulk RNA-seq gene expression data (25,000+ genes) from GBM xenolines cultured under hypoxia and normoxia
- Conducted **differential expression analysis** to identify significantly regulated genes between conditions
- Performed **Gene Set Enrichment Analysis (GSEA)** to identify pathway-level mechanisms
- Built an **interactive R Shiny application** for clinicians to explore pathway enrichment results by xenoline and condition without requiring programming expertise

### Tools

R, Bioconductor, DESeq2, fgsea, ggplot2, R Shiny
