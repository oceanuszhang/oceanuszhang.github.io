Kangaroo Rat Behavioral Field Study

Spatial dynamics and behavioral syndromes in desert kangaroo rats · SICB 2023 · Open Biology 2025

Undergraduate research at UC Irvine School of Biological Sciences under Monica Daley, PhD.

Overview

Individual animals within a population exhibit consistent behavioral differences — a phenomenon known as animal personality or behavioral syndromes. This project investigated whether body condition and sex predict exploratory behavior in desert kangaroo rats (Dipodomys deserti) in the wild.

Field Research

Led field research at Anza-Borrego Desert State Park over 15 months, collecting behavioral and morphological data on free-ranging kangaroo rats. Behavioral assays included open field exploration and novel object responses.

Statistical Analysis

  • Implemented Principal Component Analysis (PCA) to reduce dimensionality of behavioral and morphological measurements
  • Applied linear mixed-effects models to control for repeated measures and individual-level random effects
  • Bridged personality trait scores with morphological data (body mass, hindfoot length, tail length) to identify predictors of exploratory behavior

Presentations

  • Abstract accepted and presented at SICB 2023 (Society for Integrative and Comparative Biology), Austin TX
  • Paper accepted at Open Biology (2025) (Zhang & others, 2025)
